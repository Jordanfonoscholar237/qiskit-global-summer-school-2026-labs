# LinkedIn post

I have been working through the **Qiskit Global Summer School 2026** laboratory sequence, progressing from foundational quantum circuits to hardware-aware execution, noise analysis, error mitigation, optimisation and quantum chemistry.

Across Labs 0–4, I worked with:

• Qiskit patterns, Sampler and Estimator workflows  
• Bell and GHZ states, circuit depth and heavy-hex-aware transpilation  
• backend noise models, benchmarking and dynamic circuits  
• twirling, NoiseLearner and structure-aware error mitigation  
• QAOA for the number-partition problem and Pauli-correlation encoding  
• sample-based quantum diagonalisation for N₂ using Qiskit Addon SQD and PySCF

One of the most valuable lessons was that useful quantum computation is not only about writing an algorithm. It also requires careful mapping to hardware, understanding noise, preserving important physical configurations and designing reliable classical post-processing.

I have organised my work into a public portfolio with sanitised notebooks and selected visual results. All credentials, cloud identifiers, Runtime job IDs and personal contact information were removed before publication.

My long-term goal is to build expertise in quantum engineering and contribute to practical quantum technologies, research and education in Africa.
