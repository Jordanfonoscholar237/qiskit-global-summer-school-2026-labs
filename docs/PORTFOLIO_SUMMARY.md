# Portfolio summary

## Technical progression

1. **Foundations:** created circuits, used common quantum gates and followed the map–transpile–execute–post-process workflow.
2. **Hardware awareness:** studied circuit depth, connectivity constraints, heavy-hex layouts and transpilation choices.
3. **Noise:** compared ideal and noisy simulations, explored common noise channels and benchmarked backend behaviour.
4. **Mitigation:** investigated circuit randomisation, noise learning and structure-aware observable mitigation.
5. **Applications:** explored QAOA optimisation, quantum-advantage criteria and molecular ground-state estimation through SQD.

## Strong points to discuss in interviews

- You understand that hardware topology and noise affect circuit quality.
- You have used both local simulators and IBM Quantum Runtime workflows.
- You can connect quantum algorithms to classical post-processing and numerical linear algebra.
- You have experience with optimisation and quantum-chemistry applications rather than only introductory circuits.
- You documented and sanitised your work for responsible public sharing.
